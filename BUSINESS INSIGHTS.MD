## 📊 Business Intelligence – Supermarket Sales Analysis

Developed a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze supermarket sales, revenue growth, profitability, product performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

### 🎯 Key Business Performance (KPIs)

| Business Metric | Dashboard Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹2.41 Million |
| Total Profit | ₹613.50K |
| Profit Margin | 25.46% |
| Sales Growth | 9.80% |
| Total Orders | 2,000+ |
| Average Order Value | ₹1.39K |
| Total Customers | 414 |
| Customer Retention Count | 350 |
| Customer Loyalty Rate | 85% |

### 📈 Business Intelligence & Key Insights

**1. Sales & Profitability Analysis**
- Generated ₹2.41M in total revenue with ₹613.50K in profit.
- Achieved a 25.46% overall profit margin.
- Recorded 9.80% sales growth, highlighting positive business performance.
- Compared three branches, with Branch A generating approximately ₹0.97M in revenue.

**2. Product Performance Analysis**
- Identified Led Bulb as the best-selling product with 348 units sold.
- Socks ranked second with 302 units, followed by Headphones (281), Charger (280), and Extension Board (252).
- Identified Tea as the lowest-selling product.
- Electronics contributed the highest category revenue at approximately ₹0.94M (39.19%).

**3. Customer Behaviour Analysis**
- Analyzed 414 customers and approximately 2,000 orders.
- Identified 350 customers under the customer retention metric.
- Achieved an 85% customer loyalty rate.
- Identified the top customer generating approximately ₹25K in revenue.
- Analyzed customer preferences across three payment methods.

**4. Regional Sales Analysis**
- Ujjain generated approximately ₹334K in sales.
- Gwalior generated approximately ₹330K in sales.
- Compared sales performance across multiple cities to identify high-performing markets.

- Evaluated ₹2.41M in revenue to understand overall business performance.
