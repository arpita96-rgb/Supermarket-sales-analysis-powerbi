# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales performance using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, profitability, product performance, customer purchasing behavior, and sales trends.

The objective is to transform supermarket sales data into meaningful business insights that can help management make data-driven decisions, improve sales performance, and understand customer preferences.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall supermarket sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing and lowest-performing products.
- Analyze product category performance.
- Monitor sales growth and profit margins.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze customer loyalty and returning customers.
- Identify business growth opportunities.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query Editor** – Data transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
- **Data Visualization** – Business performance reporting

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of supermarket business performance.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Profit Margin
- Sales Growth

**Visualizations:**
- Revenue and Profit Trends
- Category-wise Revenue Contribution
- Branch-wise Sales and Profit Comparison
- Product Sales and Profitability Analysis
- Quantity and Sales Performance Trends

**Business Purpose:** To monitor overall business performance and identify sales and profitability trends.

### 2. Product Analysis

Analyzes product-level performance and category contribution.

**Key Metrics:**
- Best-Selling Product
- Lowest-Selling Product
- Total Product Categories
- Total Sales
- Sales Target Achievement
- Sales Gap

**Visualizations:**
- Category-wise Sales and Profit
- Product-wise Sales Performance
- Product Quantity Analysis
- Category-wise Quantity Contribution
- Top and Bottom Product Performance

**Business Purpose:** To identify profitable products, understand product demand, and improve inventory and sales strategies.

### 3. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and loyalty.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Returning Customers
- Loyal Customers

**Visualizations:**
- Customer Trends
- Gender-wise Sales Analysis
- Payment Method Analysis
- Customer Rating by Product
- Top Customers by Revenue
- City-wise Sales Performance

**Business Purpose:** To understand customer preferences, improve customer retention, and identify valuable customer segments.

## 🔍 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:
- City
- Product Category
- Product
- Membership Type
- Gender
- Order Date

These filters allow users to explore business performance across different dimensions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The dashboard is designed to help identify:

- Revenue and profit trends over time.
- Products contributing most to total sales.
- Categories generating higher profitability.
- Branches with stronger sales performance.
- Customer segments contributing to revenue.
- Returning customer and loyalty patterns.
- Differences in customer payment preferences.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing and profitable product categories.
- Develop promotional strategies for low-performing products.
- Monitor sales target achievement and sales gaps.
- Introduce customer retention and loyalty programs.
